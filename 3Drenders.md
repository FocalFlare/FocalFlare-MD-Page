# Sprout Fight
The first time I actually participated in anything Art Fight related. This honestly reminds me that Art Fight should have something like this, but neither competitive (no winners or losers) nor limited as fuck to character-involved art mediums. 

While I like adhering the art to the word prompts, the only prompts I'm focused on are the color palettes themselves. However, for those with some sort of gradient map coloring the entire image, you may see extra colors in them due to the fact that some of the color palettes don't have the contrast that I want or like.

## [2026 - TMWF](https://artfight.tumblr.com/post/811116415106138112/sprout-fight-2026)
This second set focuses on the theme of ultra Flaremon. 
* Lore-wise, "ultra" is a nickname prefix for Flaremon's ult mode, taking on a kaiju form factor where he is physically bigger than his actual size, but not actually fat despite appearing like it. He used to take on the form factor of a \[UNIDENTIFIED SPECIES\] (open species by Moopdrea & 🆔), but I've decided to have him take on a kaiju form instead; something he can actually control himself. As it's been years since I've shown any art of ultra Flaremon, I figured that the first word prompt and color prompt were both great to open this set with (I'm making this sound like I'm playing a Lost Lands set lmfao).
