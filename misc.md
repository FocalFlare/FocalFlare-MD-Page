# Art Fight Unofficial: Zen Arena `[A Focal Flare x Sernak Labs Project]`

## **Info**
All of the info about Art Fight, as well as details for each team and registering for a team, can be accessed here: [https://artfight.net](https://artfight.net)
### AF Unofficial
* **Art Fight Unofficial** is a collection of images that I would've made for the main Art Fight events, but sadly they weren't able to make their way into it. This collection could include a multitude of projects, such as (but not limited to) Zen Arena.
* **Zen Arena** is a project of Art Fight Unofficial where it's a bit similar in the "art trade" idea of Art Fight, but it's a much more casual version of it whereas Art Fight is competitive and heavily encourages efficiency amongst artists as it's a team-based "game". 
  * Possibly implied by its name, Zen Arena is a more freeform project where the art that I make for it is not heavily limited by both a prompt and the medium of just charcters. Instead, the only limitations I set for myself for each project (or image if possible) are the prompt that I pick...*and my imagination since the ability for an artist to create a particular thing can only be helped or hindered by one's own imagination of that thing lmao.*

*Art Fight Unofficial and Zen Arena is a Focal Flare x Sernak Labs project EST. 2025\. Zen Arena is neither affiliated or partnered with Art Fight nor a property of it. Art Fight is a competitive art trading platform by Blankit established in 2007 and owned by Rainy in 2025.*
